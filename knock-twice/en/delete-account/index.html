Knock Twice · an app by TiramiStack

Delete your Knock Twice account

In short: you can delete your account in the app, under Profile → Delete account, and deletion is immediate. If you no longer have the app, write to tiramistack@gmail.com from your account email address: we usually do it within 7 days, and at the latest within one month.

Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Deleting it is free and you do not have to give a reason. It cannot be undone and we cannot recover the data afterwards: if you want a copy, download it first in the app, under Profile → Download my data, or ask us by email.

In the app

  1. Open Knock Twice and sign in with your email.
  2. Go to your Profile and tap Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted and what stays.
  4. Enter the 6-digit code we email you.
  5. Tap Delete for good.

The account is deleted straight away, with no waiting period. You need an internet connection. You can do this even if you have not finished signing up, or if the app did not let you in because of your age, with Delete my data.

Changes your phone has not sent yet are lost. Afterwards you get a confirmation email, without your name or any book titles. If you did not ask for the deletion, write to us straight away at tiramistack@gmail.com.

Without the app, by email

  1. Write to tiramistack@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to the app, with the subject “Delete my account”. Nothing else is needed.
  2. If you write from a different address, we send a code to the account address and ask you to tell us what it is. We do not ask for identity documents.
  3. We delete the account usually within 7 days, and at the latest within one month of receiving your request, and send you a confirmation.

The app is only for people aged 18 and over. If you are a parent and think your child under 18 has signed up, write to the same address.

What we delete

  • your account and email address;
  • your profile: name on your door plate, date of birth, language;
  • your library: books, progress, shelves, ratings, reviews, quotes, covers and books you added by hand, including the empty markers of things you had already removed;
  • the online copy of your calendar sessions and the proof of your consent to save it;
  • if you delete in the app, also the data stored on that phone.

What stays, and for how long

  • Shared book catalogue (titles, authors, ISBNs): it stays, because it contains no data about you.
  • Server technical logs: about 1 day.
  • Email sending logs: 1 month.
  • Backups: we do not make any at the moment.
  • Deletion register: a technical account code, with no email or name, with the day and channel of the request, for 12 months; the code is then cleared and only an anonymous record stays. It is used to show that we carried out the request and to delete the account again if we ever have to restore a backup.
  • Emails we exchanged: 24 months after the last reply.
  • On your other phones: the data stays until the app connects to the internet again; the app then signs out and deletes the data. Uninstalling the app removes it at once.

All the details are in the privacy policy, section 10.
