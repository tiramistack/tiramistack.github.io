1. What Knock Twice is

Knock Twice is an app for keeping track of your books in an illustrated room: library, shelves, progress, reviews, quotes and custom covers.

It is an alpha version. It exists to test the app with a group of invited people living in Italy. It is not a finished product yet.

The app is free. This version has no payments, subscriptions or ads.
