Privacy notice of Nuvoluna

Version 2. Last updated: 10 November 2026. Applies to Nuvoluna on Android.

In short

  • Nuvoluna has no accounts, servers, ads or analytics tools, and it has no permission to use the internet.
  • What you enter stays on your phone, encrypted. I don't receive it and I don't share it with anyone.
  • You can delete everything from your phone whenever you like, with ‘Reset everything’ or by uninstalling the app.
  • The forecasts are estimates. Nuvoluna is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition.

Who I am

Nuvoluna is an app by Lorenzo Diana, an individual (a natural person). It is published on Google Play under the developer name TiramiStack, which is not a company. For questions about privacy, write to me at tiramistack@gmail.com. I haven't appointed a data protection officer, because the law does not require one.

What stays on the phone, and why

Nuvoluna saves on your phone only what you enter or choose, so that you can use the app:

  • Cycle: the start and end dates you log, and the average length of the cycle and of the period, worked out from these dates or, until there are enough of them, the one you entered. Also whether you've paused the forecast and since when, and whether you show fertile days. They are used for the calendar and the estimates.
  • Notes: the text you write on calendar days.
  • Mood and rewards: the mood of the last check-in with its date, but no history. Also the number of check-ins, the stars, the wardrobe items and the chosen character, used for the scene and the rewards. The character is an aesthetic choice: I do not use it to infer anything about you.
  • Reminders: whether they are on and at what time.
  • Widget, if you turn it on: the images of the character, with what it wears, for today and the next two days. They are kept outside the encrypted space, so that the home screen can show them, and contain nothing about the cycle, the mood or the estimates. They are deleted when you turn the widget off or with ‘Reset everything’. The widget can be seen on the home screen, even with the lock on.
  • Pregnancy mode, if you turn it on: the starting week, the kind of pictures, the day you last opened the app and your choice for Today, encrypted on the phone. It is health data: it never leaves the phone, except in a backup you export yourself. The backup is encrypted with your password. The period forecast is paused. ‘Turn off pregnancy mode’ deletes this data straight away, but not backups already exported.
  • Lock, if you turn it on: the PIN, not in plain text but turned by Argon2id into a code from which it is very hard to work back to the PIN. Also the number of wrong attempts, the time of the last one, and whether you use fingerprint or face.

Except for the widget images, the data in this list is encrypted with keys kept by the Android system. All of it stays on this phone: it isn't included in Google's automatic backups or in the transfer to a new phone.

You use this data yourself, on your phone: I don't receive it and I can't see it. The app uses it only for the features you choose, and you delete it whenever you like.

I don't ask for your name, email, age, location or contacts, and Nuvoluna doesn't read any other data on the phone.

Nothing is sent

Nuvoluna doesn't send data to me or to anyone else: it has no permission to use the internet. It doesn't contain ads, analytics tools, trackers or libraries that collect data.

When you download Nuvoluna, Google processes the data of your Google Play account under its own privacy notice. From Google Play I receive only overall statistics, such as installs and ratings, and the crash reports that Android sends to Google if you have allowed it in your phone settings. I use them only to fix bugs, on the basis of my legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). They stay in the Play Console for the time set by Google, and I do not copy them anywhere else. These reports are not designed to contain what you have entered in Nuvoluna. Reviews on Google Play are public, and so are my replies.

Notifications

The mood reminder and the notice before your period are optional and off until you turn them on. The lock screen shows only the app name and ‘Nuvi is waiting for you 🌙’ or ‘Ninna is waiting for you 🌙’. Not a word about the cycle, fertility or mood.

To send the notices even when the app is closed or after a restart, the phone keeps, outside the app's encrypted space, the day, the time and the text of each scheduled notice. Notices are scheduled for the next 14 days at most. Other apps can't read them, but someone breaking into the phone with technical tools can. If you turn on only the notice before your period, the date of the notice reveals the estimated date of your next period.

Android may also show notifications on connected devices, such as a smartwatch, and keep them in the notification history, if you have turned it on.

Backup

Backups are never automatic: if you like, you can export a .nuvoluna file. It contains your cycle, notes, mood, rewards, character, reminder settings and, if it is on, pregnancy mode. It does not contain the PIN or the lock settings.

The file is encrypted (with Argon2id and AES-256-GCM) with a password you choose, and you save it wherever you like. If you save it to a cloud service, the file stays encrypted but is held by that service, under its terms. I don't receive it and I can't recover the password, so choose a long one, for example a sentence. Anyone with the file and the password can read it.

The file stays where you saved it even after ‘Reset everything’ or uninstalling the app: it has to be deleted by hand.

Lock

The PIN lock is optional. It keeps prying eyes out when someone else has your phone, but it doesn't add encryption. It does not protect you if someone forces you to open the app or breaks into the phone with technical tools.

If you use fingerprint or face, the phone does the matching: Nuvoluna only gets a yes or a no, and does not see or save fingerprints or faces. Any fingerprint or face registered on the phone opens Nuvoluna, even if it is someone else's or was added later.

With the lock on, the preview of Nuvoluna among recent apps is covered. On Android versions before 13, covering it also blocks screenshots of the app.

Without the lock, anyone using your unlocked phone can open Nuvoluna.

Estimates, not diagnoses

The next period and, if you show them, the fertile days and ovulation are calendar estimates, worked out only from the dates you have entered. They may be wrong. Nuvoluna is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition: don't use it to avoid or seek a pregnancy. For medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, talk to a healthcare professional.

Deleting your data

  • ‘Reset everything’, in More, deletes from this phone your cycle, notes, mood, rewards, reminders, scheduled notices, pregnancy mode, widget images and PIN.
  • If you've forgotten your PIN, ‘I forgot my PIN’ deletes everything in the same way.
  • Uninstalling Nuvoluna deletes everything the app keeps inside it.
  • Exported backups stay where you saved them.

I can't delete or recover your data, because I don't have it.

If you write to me

Writing to me is optional. Emails are the only personal data I receive directly: your address and what you write in the message. I use them only to reply to you, on the basis of my legitimate interest in doing so (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). When you ask to exercise your rights, I use them to comply with my legal obligation to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

I keep them for 12 months from the last message, then delete them. The mail goes through Gmail, a Google service, under its terms. Google may also store messages outside the European Union, for example in the United States. For the United States, the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July 2023 applies (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework), in which Google LLC participates.

Please don't send me data about your cycle or your health: I don't need it to help you.

Your rights

In the app you can see all your data, correct it with ‘Edit data’, export it to a file with ‘Export’ and delete it with ‘Reset everything’, without asking anyone for anything.

For the data I receive by email, you can ask me for access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, and you can object to the processing (Arts. 15–22 GDPR). I reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, the Italian data protection authority (www.garanteprivacy.it), or with the supervisory authority of the country where you live or work, or where you believe the infringement took place.

Nuvoluna does not make automated decisions that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you (Art. 22 GDPR).

You can first make a complaint to me, at the address above: within 30 days, I'll confirm that I have received it. You can then contact the Information Commission (ico.org.uk). For messages stored in the United States, the UK Extension to the Data Privacy Framework applies. Nuvoluna does not make automated decisions within the meaning of Arts. 22A–22D UK GDPR.

You can ask me for access to your data, for your data to be handed over to you, for rectification or for erasure: I reply within 30 days. For messages stored in the United States, the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework applies, in which Google LLC participates.
