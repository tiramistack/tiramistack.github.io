What Nuvoluna does

It's a diary. The next period and, if you show them, the fertile days and ovulation are calendar estimates, worked out only from the dates you enter: the next period from the average of your last cycles, ovulation 14 days before the next estimated period. They may be wrong, especially with cycles that vary a lot or with few dates entered. Pregnancy mode is a picture game, the same for everyone: it doesn't measure anything.
